# CIRP: First-time iPhone buyers don’t feel the need to buy the latest, greatest models
Posted by [Dennis Sellers](https://www.mactech.com/author/dennis-sellers/ "Posts by Dennis Sellers") | Jul 23, 2026 | [MacTech News](https://www.mactech.com/category/mactech-news/) | 
A small but steady number of iPhone buyers switch from Android. Last week Consumer Intelligence Research Partners ([CIRP](https://www.cirpllc.com)) updated its switching (and loyalty) analysis to show [around 12% of iPhone buyers came from the Android ](https://substack.com/redirect/6f5314fc-8205-49be-b2b0-a3333db13238?j=eyJ1IjoiMjBocTFyIn0.zXt2gqCVJOAjh7ZifWZj_5BHoTzAGnLuYhFzVSIa1fA)operating system. 
But what iPhones do they buy? The expectation is that first-time iPhone buyers will be satisfied with any iPhone. They don’t feel the need or perhaps fully understand the appeal of the newest, leading-edge iPhone models. Just getting iMessage and FaceTime is enough. 
CIRP says the data supports that logic. In the six-month period ending in March 2026, 68% of Android switchers bought one of the new flagship iPhone 17 models: 17, 17 Air, 17 Pro, or 17 Pro Max (Chart 1). In comparison, 80% of legacy iPhone owners who bought a new iPhone chose one of those models.
