Crossref abstract: This paper investigates the dynamics of factor crowding within quantitative equity portfolio management. As factor investing gains popularity, the symptoms of crowding have become increasingly pronounced. The paper deepens understanding of risk-return dynamics associated with factor crowding from cross-sectional and temporal perspectives. Cross-sectionally, the authors hypothesize about drivers of return dynamics in the context of factor crowding and how they interact with alpha and risk factors, substantiating conjectures with empirical evidence and highlighting common misconceptions among quantitative practitioners. Temporally, the paper documents how factor crowding has evolved over time and finds that susceptibility to crowding is not uniform across factors -- factors with higher barriers to entry maintain performance longer, i.e., are less prone to crowding-driven decay. Authors Ronald Hua and Clare Sun.
