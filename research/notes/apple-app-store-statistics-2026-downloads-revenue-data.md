## **Apple App Store Statistics: Deep Dive**
### **1.**Apple App Store Statistics:** Download Statistics**
The App Store has grown from 500 apps at launch in 2008 to approximately 1.86–2.42 million apps available today.
Annual download volume on the App Store reached approximately **37 billion in 2025** , per electroiq and Sensor Tower estimates. 
For context, Google Play recorded an estimated 136 billion downloads over the same period — roughly 3.7× more than the App Store. [Read our complete report on Google Play statistics here →](https://www.getpanto.ai/blog/google-play-statistics)
That gap reflects Android’s broader global footprint in lower-income markets, not higher per-user engagement. iOS users download fewer apps but generate significantly more revenue per download.
#### ****Apple App Store Statistics:** Download Volume Over Time**  
| **Year**  | **Estimated Downloads**  |  
| --- | --- |  
| 2024  | ~36 billion  |  
| 2025  | ~37 billion  |  
| 2026 (projected)  | ~38 billion  |  
The US generated **8.5 billion App Store downloads in 2025** (+14.42% YoY), followed by China at approximately 6.6 billion downloads. Japan posted consistent growth at +13.98% YoY. 
The UK saw monthly active users surge **+78.39% YoY** — the highest MAU growth of any major App Store market in 2025. Vietnam led the top 10 by download growth rate at **+15.50% YoY**.
### **2. Apple App Store Statistics: Revenue Statistics**
Revenue is where the App Store’s structural advantage is clearest. iOS generates substantially more consumer spending per download than Google Play, across nearly every category.
Maintaining that monetization advantage requires consistent quality across devices, making [iOS app testing](https://www.getpanto.ai/products/ios-app-testing) an essential part of every App Store release cycle.
**Consumer spending on the App Store reached $117.6 billion in 2025** , a 13.6% increase over 2024. Of that total:
  * **$51.9 billion** came from games
  * **$64.8 billion** came from non-gaming apps — the first year non-gaming outpaced gaming in absolute terms


The broader ecosystem figure is considerably larger. 
Apple’s June 2026 report, produced by economists at Analysis Group, measured **$1.4 trillion in total developer billings and sales in 2025**.
This encompasses retail, travel, food delivery, and other commerce conducted through iOS apps. 
Physical goods and services alone accounted for **$1.1 trillion** of that total.

This illustrates how sharply monetization diverges between the two platforms.
### **3.**Apple App Store Statistics:** Usage and App Categories**
Games remain the dominant category by both downloads and revenue. 
By download share, games account for approximately **30–41% of all App Store downloads** , followed by Photo and Video (~10%), Shopping (~8%), and Entertainment (~7%).
By revenue, games generated **$51.9 billion** in consumer spending in 2025, representing approximately 44% of total direct consumer spending.
Business of Apps notes non-gaming apps crossed $64.8 billion in the same period, outpacing gaming in absolute terms for the first time.
AI apps marked the most significant category-level shift in 2025. 
**More than 40 of the top 100 App Store apps featured consumer-facing AI capabilities** , and those apps collectively showed stronger billing growth than other top-100 apps.

### **8. Apple App Store Statistics: Market Trends and Regulatory Context**
**AI is reshaping App Store category economics.** Over 40 of the top 100 App Store apps featured consumer-facing AI capabilities in 2025, and those apps showed stronger billing growth than the rest of the top 100. 
As AI-powered applications become more complex, many development teams are adopting [AI automation testing](https://www.getpanto.ai/products/ai-automation-testing) to validate dynamic user journeys and AI-assisted workflows before deployment.
Apple integrated ChatGPT directly into iOS via Visual Intelligence and iOS 26 expands on-device AI across the platform.
**The regulatory environment shifted materially in 2025.**
Following the April 2025 contempt ruling in Epic v. Apple, US developers can link to external payments at **0% Apple commission** — the first time no-commission external linking has been available on iOS in the US.
The Ninth Circuit upheld the contempt finding in December 2025. Apple was separately fined **€500 million by the European Commission** in April 2025 for non-compliance with the EU Digital Markets Act (DMA). 
Despite the DMA requiring Apple to allow alternative app stores in the EU since March 2024, **EU alternative marketplace adoption rates remain below 3%** as of 2025.
**VisionOS is an early-stage but growing category.** visionOS apps for Apple Vision Pro exceeded **4,200 AR/VR titles** by Q1 2026.
### **Conclusion**
The Apple App Store generated $117.6 billion in direct consumer spending in 2025 and facilitated $1.4 trillion in total ecosystem transactions, nearly three times the scale of 2019. 
The platform serves over 850 million weekly users, and non-gaming apps crossed gaming in absolute spending for the first time, while AI-powered apps posted the strongest billing growth of any segment in the top 100.
What these figures collectively signal is that the App Store has matured from a software distribution channel into a full commerce and advertising infrastructure. 
For developers and market observers, the App Store’s ability to generate nearly 2.5× the consumer spending of Google Play from a fraction of the downloads is the defining characteristic of the iOS platform.
### **FAQs**
#### **Q: How many users does the Apple App Store have?**
**A:** Apple reports that the App Store serves **more than 850 million average weekly users** across **175 countries and regions** , making it one of the world’s largest digital app marketplaces.
#### **Q: How much revenue does the Apple App Store generate?**
**A:** Consumer spending on the App Store reached approximately **$117.6 billion in 2025**. Looking beyond app purchases alone, Apple says its developer ecosystem generated **$1.4 trillion** in billings and sales across physical goods, digital goods, services, and in-app advertising.
#### **Q: How many apps are available on the Apple App Store?**
**A:** Current estimates range from roughly **1.86 million** to **2.42 million apps**. The difference comes from how research firms count regional variants, enterprise apps, hidden listings, and inactive titles.
#### **Q: How many apps are downloaded from the App Store each year?**
**A:** Industry estimates from Sensor Tower and electroiq put App Store downloads at approximately **37 billion in 2025**. Apple does not publish an official annual download figure.
#### **Q: What commission does Apple charge on App Store purchases?**
**A:** Apple’s standard App Store commission is **30%**. Developers earning under **$1 million annually** qualify for the **15%** Small Business Program. In the United States, developers can now link users to external payment options with **0% Apple commission** following the April 2025 _Epic v. Apple_ ruling, while developers in the EU are subject to Apple’s Core Technology Commission and alternative distribution terms.
#### **Q: What are the most popular App Store categories?**
**A:** **Games** remain the largest category by both downloads and consumer spending, generating approximately **$51.9 billion** in 2025. However, **non-gaming apps collectively generated even more revenue** , reaching **$64.8 billion**. Among the top-performing apps, AI-powered applications were the fastest-growing segment by consumer spending.
#### **Q: How does the Apple App Store compare with Google Play?**
**A:** The App Store generated approximately **$117.6 billion** in consumer spending during 2025, compared with **$49.2 billion** for Google Play. That gave Apple roughly **70.5% of combined two-store revenue** despite accounting for only about **21% of total downloads** , highlighting the significantly higher spending per iOS user.
