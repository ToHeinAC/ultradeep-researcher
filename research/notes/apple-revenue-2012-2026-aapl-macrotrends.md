## Apple Revenue 2012-2026 | AAPL



Apple annual/quarterly revenue history and growth rate from 2012 to 2026. Revenue can be defined as the amount of money a company receives from its customers in exchange for the sales of goods or services. Revenue is the top line item on an income statement from which all costs and expenses are subtracted to arrive at net income.
