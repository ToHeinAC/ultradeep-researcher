# Step 10 draft angles

## Draft A
STRONGEST THESIS (dominant evidence direction). Argue that the durability frame materially changes the picture: ~20-28% of total gross profit (47-67% of Services GP) sits in contingent or repriceable streams (App Store commissions, Google TAC); commission erosion is durable in direction; FY2026 hardware growth is price/mix-led on contracting underlying margins; the AI position was revealed, not chosen. Therefore the 34.6x headline multiple overstates earnings quality by ~2-5 turns, and the 2-5y return case leans on multiple support rather than on durable fundamentals. Commit hard; engage the bull rebuttals explicitly.

## Draft B
STEELMAN CONTRARIAN (the minority bull view, defended seriously). Argue that the contingent share is less dangerous than its size suggests: TAC is a repriceable annuity whose modal outcome is continuation (Mehta permits payment; no rival bidder but also no ban); Apple's own remand ask (15/10/5%) still leaves meaningful fees; preference-based loyalty survived the DMA natural experiment; Apple gains unit share in the memory shock; renting Gemini while R&D rises is capital-efficient; payment-rail disruption from crypto is refuted by the adoption evidence; modem/silicon in-sourcing adds margin. Where the evidence ultimately beats the bull case, concede precisely and say what remains standing.

## Draft C
SYNTHESIS / BOUNDARY CONDITIONS. Argue that both bull and bear capture part of the truth and specify WHEN each holds: differentiate haircuts by stream (small for TAC, larger for commissions); decompose the multiple into earned vs passive-flow/factor/duration artefact with ranges and method; compute the services CAGR required to absorb a hardware hangover (-4% to -6% absorbable, -8% not); lay out the scenario tree with timelines (D.C. Cir. 26-5023, SCOTUS 25-1311, Epic remand, DOJ v. Apple, DMA) and name the leading indicators that would move the verdict. Test the reader's two priors as conditional claims (payments: mechanism refuted, direction affirmed; AI: origin revealed, current economics conditionally efficient).
