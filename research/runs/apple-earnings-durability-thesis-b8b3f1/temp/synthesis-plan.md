# Synthesis plan

## Core thesis (1-2 sentences)
Roughly one-fifth to one-quarter of Apple's gross profit (19.7–28.3%; 46.7–67.2% of Services GP) sits in two streams whose price a court or regulator sets or can reset. The durability frame therefore survives falsification (~1.5–4 turns off 34.6x). But the brief's risk labels are inverted: Google TAC is the legally durable, rate-compressing annuity, and App Store commissions are the stream already eroding. The larger unearned part of the premium is not in the court files. It is the market paying a subscription-like multiple for cyclical, price-led hardware profit. So the evidence favours the bull case on the *existence* of earnings and a mild bear case on the *multiple*.

## The strongest argumentative beats
1. **Taxonomy inversion** — all three drafts, independently. "Binary" TAC won its existence question (payment ban rejected; 6-year regime; appeal is narrow and procedural, so a remand is the typical result). "Contingent" commissions are already eroding: stay denied, remand at ~0–15% vs 27%, US commission revenue −18% YTD (Appfigures). Load-bearing because it reverses the valuation treatment the brief proposed.
2. **Differentiated falsification test** — A/B/C converge. A small TAC haircut plus a large commission haircut gives ~31–33x (−1.5 to −4 turns; up to ~5 on an operating-income basis). Material, not noise, but smaller than the naive 2–5 flat test implies.
3. **Inverse test: implied hardware multiple** — Draft C, the report's most original beat. Assign generous multiples to annuity (38–45x), TAC (29x) and commissions (19x). Solving 34.6x for hardware then gives ~35.5–37.7x on cyclical, price-led, memory-inflated hardware GP. This shows where the premium actually rests.
4. **Services cushion erodes itself** — Drafts A and C (dynamic table from C). Statically, 12–16% Services growth absorbs a −4 to −6% Products hangover, not −8%. But if commissions stagnate, the rest of Services must grow ~21–22% to hold 12%; if commissions fall 10% p.a., tolerance shrinks to ~−2%. The timing risks coincide.
5. **Probability-weighted TAC is still a declining asset** — Draft A, clearly labelled as a construction. 50/30/20 weights give an expected 2031 TAC of ~$19.7B vs a ~$26B run-rate, even though the modal outcome is "nothing changes". Reconciles "legally durable" with "needs a haircut".
6. **Google on both sides** — A/B/C, labelled as inference. The court found TAC disincentivised Apple from building search capability; Apple now rents Gemini from the same payer under the same remedy regime. Efficient rent can become revealed dependency.
7. **Reader priors as conditional verdicts** — B/C framing, A's force.
   - Prior I: origin revealed, not chosen (high confidence). Current economics are a capital-STRUCTURE substitution that is efficient under three conditions (inference costs keep falling; the interface stays on the device; Google stays a willing counterparty).
   - Prior II: the crypto/AI-rails mechanism is refuted (stablecoin cards <0.1% of network volume and riding Visa rails; no agentic-payment adoption data). The direction is affirmed: checkout rents erode through courts and the DMA.
   - TAC is never mixed with payments.

## Where drafts disagreed
- **Size of the valuation gap:** A −1.8 to −4.1; B −1.5 to −3.6; C −1.4 to −3.6 (GP), −5.3 (OI). **Commit to** band ~1.5–4 turns on GP basis, central ~2.5–3, up to ~5 on OI basis. The drafts converge; state that as a finding from independent angles.
- **Is the rest of the premium over 28x earned?**
  - B: mostly earned (small flow, bounded haircut).
  - A: exposed/unearned (price-led hardware, eroding cushion, no flow floor).
  - **Commit to C:** the residual ~4–7 turns is an expectation re-rating resting on three assumptions (durable services, durable high hardware prices, intact contingent terms). Current evidence weakens the second and third (price-led growth, ex-refund GM contracting, commissions eroding). Hence mildly bear on the multiple, bull on existence. B's point stays visible: short duration, strong FCF, and the premium formed against high real yields rather than on rate tailwinds.
- **Main 2-year risk: courts vs margin/multiple normalisation.**
  - B: margin/multiple normalisation dominates.
  - A: courts and erosion.
  - **Commit:** both, via C's calendar. For a 2-year holder the catalysts are the remand rate, Services vs 12%, and ex-refund GM vs 46.5%. TAC only enters after ~2028. B is right that the D.C. Circuit is not the first thing to watch.
- **Prior I forward:**
  - B: "mostly right forward".
  - A: "rejected in present form".
  - **Commit to C:** backward wrong, forward conditionally tenable, not an "advantage" in itself.
- **Commission haircut:** see Conflict 8.
- **TAC 2031 value:** A's EV ~$19.7B vs B/C's base path $27–33B. **Not contradictory:** base path (modal) vs probability-weighted EV. Present both, clearly labelled.
